Common questions

SMS shows as unavailable

Put your own phone number in ⚙︎ Settings: MeetChat needs it to send SMS, and shares it with the people in your chats so they can text you. A phone with no SIM card can't send SMS at all.

Messages don't arrive over the internet

Everyone in a chat must use the same relay server. New chats take the server set in ⚙︎ Settings → Internet Relay and carry it in their invite.

Phones nearby don't find each other

Bluetooth must be on and MeetChat open on both phones. Come within 10–50 m. Two iPhones also meet over Wi-Fi; an iPhone and an Android phone need Bluetooth.

I bought the Full Version on another phone

⚙︎ Settings → Full Version → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

My radio doesn't send encrypted chats

If it's in licensed (amateur radio) mode, MeetChat doesn't send encrypted messages on it, because amateur radio rules don't allow encryption. Turn licensed mode off in the Meshtastic app.

Move to a new phone

Install MeetChat and join your chats again with their QR codes or invite links from someone in them.
