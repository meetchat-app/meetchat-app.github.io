MeetChat Manual Support Privacy Terms

Keep talking when the network doesn't.

After a storm, an earthquake or a long blackout, calls and data often stop working. MeetChat keeps your group talking by whatever still works — text message, internet, phones nearby or Meshtastic® radio — and every message is end-to-end encrypted on your phone first.

Read the manual Get support

Four ways through

Text message, internet, phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with no network at all, and Meshtastic LoRa radio for kilometres.

Join with a QR code

No account and no phone number needed. Show the chat's QR code or send its link; iPhone and Android in the same chat.

Like the chats you know

Photos, files, your location, reactions, replies, edits — and deleting a message for everyone.

A map for the group

Where everyone is, how they are (OK, Danger, Help), and pins for shelter, water, first aid and meeting points.

Private by design

Each chat has its own key, made on the phone. The relay, the radios and the phones that carry messages can't read them.

Free, then one payment

One chat free, with no limit on people or messages. The Full Version, a single payment, unlocks as many chats as you like.

Not an emergency service

MeetChat helps your group stay in touch, but it doesn't replace the emergency services. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 (or your local emergency number) whenever you can.
